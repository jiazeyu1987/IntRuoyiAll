## Root Cause

- The `update-original` contract accepted client-owned `modifiedByUserId`, `revisionSignatureUserId`, `revisionSignatureId`, and `revisionSignatureSnapshot`, and the service only checked ID equality/duplicate use rather than generating a signature from the current authenticated actor.
- The fix keeps client input to business payload, change reason, signature password, and diff details; the controller injects `getLoginUserId()`, and the service calls `MesProBatchRecordExecutionSignatureService.recordFieldChangeSignature` with a revision challenge hash before persisting returned signature evidence.
